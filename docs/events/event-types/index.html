Mission Classifications

Mission Classification

🟢 CASUAL

Low combat intensity with minimal coordination required.
Suitable for casual play.
Operational risk is virtually zero and mistakes carry no penalty.
AFK permissible.

Mission Classification

🟡 OPERATIONAL

Moderate combat intensity.
Tactical errors are acceptable. While mistakes will impede operational efficiency and slow progress, they do not present a critical threat to overall mission success.

Mission Classification

🔍 RECON

High combat intensity paired with an exploratory focus.
Tactical errors and minimal progress are expected as operatives map mechanics and test strategies.
Success is measured by intelligence gathered rather than mission completion.

Mission Classification

🔴 TACTICAL

Maximum combat intensity requiring high-precision execution.
All operatives must satisfy prerequisites prior to drop.
Unearned carries will not be permitted, and failure to execute mechanics will result in consecutive wipes.
